With view-only content-control tags drawn, Backspace or Delete beside a tag selects that tag's control whole, painted as selected, and the next press removes it with what it holds; the key never takes a character on the far side of a drawn tag. With the tags drawn, a key in a control showing its prompt removes the control at once; without them a key keeps Word's rule. A wrapper locked against deletion is refused, as before.
